Who We Are

Driven by Purpose

Our Mission

Financial Clarity for Every Chapter of Life

Mc Arthur Financial Planning Services was founded on a simple but powerful belief: South Africans deserve financial advice that is free from bias, conflicts of interest and sales pressure.

Our team brings together decades of combined expertise in investment management, financial planning, tax structuring and estate law - all channelled into a single, coherent service offering for each client.

We work with individuals, families, business owners and professionals across all wealth stages - from those just beginning to build assets to established high-net-worth clients seeking sophisticated portfolio solutions.

  • Independent advice, never influenced by product providers or commissions
  • Long-term orientation over short-term gains or quick fixes
  • Radical transparency in fees, strategy and communication
  • Personalised attention - no two clients receive the same plan
  • Proactive partnership, not a transactional relationship
Our Story

Our Services

Everything You Need,
Under One Roof

Five core service areas, delivered by specialists - all coordinated around your unique financial situation.

Service 01

Investment Management

We build and actively manage investment portfolios designed to grow your wealth over the long term. Unlike bank-linked advisers, we select from the full market of investment products - choosing only what's best for you.

Every portfolio is constructed around your risk profile, investment horizon, tax situation and return objectives. We monitor performance continuously and rebalance proactively.

  • Full market access - not limited to any single provider
  • Risk-profiled portfolio construction
  • Regular performance reviews and reporting
  • Proactive rebalancing through market cycles
  • Local, offshore and multi-asset class exposure
📈 Investment Management
Service 02

Financial Planning

Comprehensive financial planning goes far beyond investments. We map your entire financial picture - income, expenses, debt, insurance, retirement and tax - into a cohesive, actionable roadmap.

Whether you're planning for retirement, a major life event, or simply want clarity over your finances, we provide the structure and guidance you need to move forward with confidence.

  • Comprehensive financial needs analysis
  • Retirement planning and income projections
  • Tax-efficient structuring strategies
  • Insurance and risk cover assessments
  • Education and major event planning
🗺️ Financial Planning
Service 03

Estate Planning

A well-structured estate ensures your wealth passes to your loved ones efficiently, minimising taxes, delays and family disputes. We work with qualified legal specialists to draft or review your will, set up trusts and ensure your estate is properly structured.

We help clients plan for succession, protect assets from creditors and ensure minor beneficiaries are properly provided for.

  • Will drafting and regular review
  • Trust formation and administration
  • Estate duty and CGT planning
  • Beneficiary nomination optimisation
  • Business succession planning
⚖️ Estate Planning
Service 04

International Asset Management

For clients with offshore assets or those wishing to diversify globally, we offer specialist international portfolio management and structuring advice. In today's interconnected world, a portion of your wealth should be positioned to benefit from global opportunities.

We navigate the regulatory requirements for SA residents holding offshore assets - ensuring full compliance while maximising international growth potential.

  • Offshore portfolio construction and management
  • SARS exchange control compliance
  • Foreign trust and company structuring
  • Currency risk management
  • Emigration and financial emigration planning
🌍 International Assets
Service 05

Our relationship doesn't end once your plan is in place. We provide ongoing advisory support through regular review meetings, ad-hoc consultations and proactive communication when market conditions or regulations change.

Think of us as your personal financial sounding board - available when you need guidance on a major financial decision.

  • Scheduled annual and bi-annual reviews
  • Ad-hoc financial decision support
  • Life event guidance (marriage, divorce, inheritance)
  • Market update reports and commentary
  • Tax season assistance and coordination

How We Work

The ABC Method

A structured, proven three-stage process that transforms financial complexity into lasting clarity.

A
Step 01
Phase One

Analyse

Before we can recommend anything, we need to truly understand your world. This phase is about deep discovery - your current financial position, your aspirations, your fears and the constraints you're working within.

We believe most financial mistakes begin with incomplete information. Our thorough analysis phase ensures we have the full picture before making a single recommendation.

Financial Discovery Session

A comprehensive initial meeting covering assets, liabilities, income, expenses, insurance and existing investments.

Risk Profiling

A structured assessment of your tolerance for investment risk, time horizon and capacity to absorb market volatility.

Goal Mapping

Identifying and prioritising your short, medium and long-term financial goals - retirement, education, property, legacy.

Life Stage Review

Considering where you are in life - career stage, family situation, health and expected future changes.

B
Step 02
Phase Two

Build

With a complete picture of your financial life, we design a bespoke strategy. This is where our independence pays dividends - we are free to select the best instruments from the entire market, without any obligation to any single provider.

Every element of your plan is selected for one reason: it's the right fit for you.

Investment Portfolio Design

Constructing an asset-allocated portfolio aligned to your risk profile, across local and offshore markets.

Tax & Estate Structuring

Ensuring your wealth is held in the most tax-efficient structures, with a clear estate plan in place.

Product Selection

Independent selection of investment vehicles, retirement annuities, living annuities, ETFs and unit trusts - best of market only.

Strategy Presentation

We present your personalised plan in a clear, jargon-free document - explaining every decision and its rationale.

C
Step 03
Phase Three

Implement & Monitor

A great plan only has value if it's executed properly and maintained over time. We handle the full implementation process - opening accounts, transferring assets and coordinating with third parties - so the burden doesn't fall on you.

Once live, we monitor your portfolio actively and meet with you regularly to assess progress, make adjustments and ensure your strategy continues to serve your goals.

Full Implementation

End-to-end execution of your financial plan - account openings, transfers and administrative coordination handled for you.

Ongoing Performance Monitoring

Continuous tracking of portfolio performance against benchmarks and your personal objectives.

Regular Review Meetings

Scheduled annual and bi-annual reviews to assess your plan, review performance and incorporate life changes.

Proactive Adjustments

Rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting and strategic shifts when market conditions or your circumstances change.
